01Background

I keep licensing and credentialing registers accurate, and I build the reporting that finds the gaps before an auditor does.

Most recently I ran license and contract compliance for a property and casualty claims TPA — a weekly audit confirming that every claim went to an adjuster with a current contract and a license valid in the state of loss, across a contracted field workforce of 250 or more.

02Selected work

  • Built the weekly license and contract compliance audit from nothing, documented the procedure, and ran it. It caught two to five noncompliant assignments a week early on. I drove that to near zero.
  • Onboarded 250+ contracted adjusters and appraisers from signed contract through system access, coordinating provisioning across IT, accounting, and claims.
  • Reconciled adjuster records across three systems that disagreed with each other, correcting inconsistencies at volume until each contractor matched system to system.
  • Ran a claims queue across three Florida carrier accounts at up to 150 files a day, sustaining 60-hour weeks through catastrophe response after Hurricanes Helene and Milton.

03Currently building

I administer a Debian Linux server and I am building the licensing register I used to keep by hand: a MariaDB schema with referential integrity constraints, a Python data layer, and a daily Excel exception report that lands on a cron schedule.

It answers two questions every morning. Who expires in the next sixty days, and is anyone still marked active on a license that already lapsed.
